# Historical Hikvision research

`linux-lab/` contains the former CLI authentication and Wine/SDK experiments.
The standalone viewer was moved out to `../linux/`. Production/development apps
v1, v2, v3 and linux do not import this directory. Historical paths and reports
are preserved as evidence; they do not establish current deployment state.
SDK archives, private state, network captures and runtime logs remain excluded
by the research ignore rules. No research credentials were inspected or exported.
